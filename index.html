About Skills Building Security

Hi, I am Ebole Junior, founder of Skills Building Security. Our mission is to empower learners in Buea and Kumba with practical security and IT skills.

Skills Building Security is a training and membership program that provides professional guidance, hands-on learning, and support for people who want to build a strong career in cybersecurity and digital safety.

We combine structured learning, mentorship, and community-building to help you achieve your goals faster.
